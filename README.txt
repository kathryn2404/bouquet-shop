PETAL & POETRY — FLOWER BOUQUET SHOP WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. An internet connection is needed for the online flower photographs.

CUSTOMIZE
- Change the shop name, text, prices, and contact email in index.html.
- Replace the image URLs with photographs of your own bouquets.
- The product category filters and demo shopping bag work in the browser.
- This is a front-end demo. It does not process payments or send real orders.

Built as a responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript website.
